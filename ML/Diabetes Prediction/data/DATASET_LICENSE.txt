Diabetes model training data

Pima Indians Diabetes Database (768 women of Pima Indian heritage, age 21+)
Originally from the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK).
Smith, J.W., Everhart, J.E., Dickson, W.C., Knowler, W.C., Johannes, R.S. (1988). Using the ADAP
learning algorithm to forecast the onset of diabetes mellitus. Proc. Annual Symposium on Computer
Application in Medical Care, 261-265.
Distributed by the UCI Machine Learning repository; Kaggle copy "uciml/pima-indians-diabetes-database"
(license listed there: CC0 1.0 Public Domain).

Note: zeros in Glucose, BloodPressure, SkinThickness, Insulin and BMI mean "not measured".
train_diabetes_model.py treats them as missing values.
